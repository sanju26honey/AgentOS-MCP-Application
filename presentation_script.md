## 🎤 Detailed Spoken Script

### 🎬 PART 1: Slide Presentation (Slides 1–8 | 0:00 – 2:00)

#### Slide 1: Cover & Intro
⏱️ **Time:** 0:00 – 0:15 (15s) | 📺 **Visual:** *Title Slide 01 — Universal AI-Commerce Adapter for Razorpay Merchants*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**
> *"Good morning judges! My name is Sanju, and today I am presenting the **Universal AI-Commerce Adapter for Razorpay Merchants** under Track 1: AI Growth & Agentic Commerce. Our mission is simple: let every Razorpay merchant become AI-discoverable, while keeping every autonomous purchase secure, approved, and auditable."*

---

#### Slide 2: The Broken Moment
⏱️ **Time:** 0:15 – 0:30 (15s) | 📺 **Visual:** *Slide 02 — Ask → Find → Break → Lose*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**
> *"Shoppers are increasingly asking AI assistants like ChatGPT and Claude to find products. But when an AI finds a product, checkout breaks—the buyer is forced to leave the AI, open a browser, search manually, and fill out forms. Merchants lose this intent-rich conversion. Agentic commerce needs a trusted action layer, not just another search bar."*

---

#### Slide 3: Proposed Solution
⏱️ **Time:** 0:30 – 0:45 (15s) | 📺 **Visual:** *Slide 03 — Vendor API (23 Endpoints) vs MCP Server (7 Tools)*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**
> *"Our solution provides two secure interfaces: a 23-endpoint Vendor API for merchant management, and a 7-tool MCP server for AI agents. It features **Dual Controls**: transactions below configured limits complete automatically; transactions above merchant or buyer limits require explicit Human-in-the-Loop approval."*

---

#### Slide 4: Merchant Side Attachment
⏱️ **Time:** 0:45 – 1:00 (15s) | 📺 **Visual:** *Slide 04 — Merchant Side (Custom Stack, Python & Node)*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**
> *"On the merchant side, attachment takes minutes. Developers simply paste 1 line of router middleware into any Python, Node, or Next.js backend. Existing storefronts gain standard AI capabilities—inventory sync, product discovery, policy checks, AI cross-sell, and Razorpay order capture—with zero website rewrite."*

---

#### Slide 5: Buyer Side Experience
⏱️ **Time:** 1:00 – 1:15 (15s) | 📺 **Visual:** *Slide 05 — Buyer Side (Connect → Ask → Evaluate → Buy)*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**
> *"On the buyer side, shoppers simply add the Razorpay MCP endpoint to Claude, ChatGPT, or Gemini. Once connected, the user asks their AI in plain language, and the agent evaluates catalog stock, checks budget policies, and completes purchases while remaining strictly bounded by the buyer’s approval limit."*

---

#### Slide 6: Network Vision
⏱️ **Time:** 1:15 – 1:30 (15s) | 📺 **Visual:** *Slide 06 — Network Vision (Buyer AI Agent ↔ MCP Server + Router ↔ Merchants)*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**
> *"Our MCP adapter acts as the foundational node engine. As Razorpay merchants attach MCP endpoints, Razorpay can aggregate them into a federated AI marketplace—ranking products, checking delivery ETAs, and routing the transaction to the optimal vendor."*

---

#### Slide 7: Live Flow & Trust by Design
⏱️ **Time:** 1:30 – 1:45 (15s) | 📺 **Visual:** *Slide 07 — Live Flow (Search → Cross-sell → Guardrail → Approve → Pay)*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**
> *"Here is our core flow: Search, cross-sell recommendation, policy guardrail check, human approval if limits are exceeded, and payment capture. Every payment signature is verified, and real-time audit telemetry preserves an immutable decision trail on the merchant dashboard."*

---

#### Slide 8: Future Enhancements
⏱️ **Time:** 1:45 – 2:00 (15s) | 📺 **Visual:** *Slide 08 — Future Enhancements (Inventory Sync, Smarter Selection, Commerce Depth)*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**

#### Slide 9: Demonstration Intro

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**

#### Scene 2: AI Growth Engine & Smart Cross-Sell
⏱️ **Time:** 2:30 – 3:00 (30s) | 📺 **Visual:** *Agent execution trace showing `get_smart_upsell` output*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**
> *"In Scene 2, watch how our adapter actively drives growth. The agent calls `get_smart_upsell`. The engine evaluates complementary items and calculates a 15% AI Bundle Offer for an RFID Leather Wallet. Because the user's budget allows it, the adapter offers this cross-sell deal, boosting the merchant's Average Order Value (AOV) automatically."*

---

#### Scene 3: Policy Engine Guardrails & Limit Breach
⏱️ **Time:** 3:00 – 3:30 (30s) | 📺 **Visual:** *Order draft creation exceeding the ₹5,000 policy limit*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**
> *"In Scene 3, we test our safety guardrails. We add a Minimalist Chronograph Watch priced at ₹3,899, bringing the order total to ₹8,398.00. 
> 
> When the agent invokes `create_order`, the Policy Engine pre-flights 4 deterministic guardrails: Price Integrity, Inventory Lock, Velocity Limit, and Transaction Cap. Because ₹8,398 exceeds the merchant's ₹5,000 autonomous limit, the transaction is safely paused."*

---

#### Scene 4: Human Approval Drawer & Razorpay Capture
⏱️ **Time:** 3:30 – 4:00 (30s) | 📺 **Visual:** *1-Click Step-Up Approval Drawer sliding up on screen $\rightarrow$ Click Approve*

> **[SPOKEN SCRIPT]**
> *"In Scene 4, notice how human control is preserved. The 1-Click Step-Up Approval Drawer slides up on screen, presenting the verified order summary. 
> 
> As the user, I click **'Approve Order'**. Instantly, the HMAC authorization token is verified, `confirm_payment` executes, and funds are captured on Razorpay rails with full stock commitment!"*

---

#### Scene 5: Real-Time Telemetry & Audit Logs

Merchants retain 100% visibility and regulatory auditability over every autonomous AI interaction."*
